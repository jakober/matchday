Privacy Policy

Matchday – Who's In? · Last updated: 4 September 2026

Matchday is an app for a group to share fixture lists and tell each other who's coming. It processes only what that requires. No ads, no tracking, no selling of data.

Legal basis

Processing serves to provide the app, i.e. performance of the usage relationship (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Whoever invites another person enters their name and address; the invitation email contains only the group name and the code, no free text. The invitee may ignore the invitation; the details are then kept until the invitation is deleted and used for nothing else (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

What does not happen

  • No advertising and no cross-app tracking
  • No sharing or selling of data for advertising
  • No location data, no contacts, no access to photos
  • No analysis of usage behaviour

Recipients

  • Supabase (Supabase Inc.) – database, accounts, sign-in and server functions. Servers located in Ireland (EU).
  • Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France) – delivery of confirmation and invitation emails. Receives recipient address and email content.
  • Apple and Google – delivery of notifications. They receive the device push token and the text of each notification.
  • calovo and fixtur.es – sources of fixture lists. The app fetches public calendar files from them; the service sees the device's IP address, nothing else.
  • TheSportsDB – source of club crests. Queried by the server only, with team names; no user data is sent.

Who can see what

Within a group, all members see each other's name, colour and answers including reasons. The group creator additionally sees whether a member sees all or only highlighted matches. Nothing is visible outside your own group; the database enforces this with row-level access rules. Email addresses and push tokens are not readable by any member, not even the group creator.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept as long as the account exists. Signing out in the app removes all local data from the device. To delete the account and all associated data on the server, a short message to the address above is enough; deletion happens within 14 days. When someone leaves or is removed from a group, their answers disappear from the group.

Your rights

Access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection under the GDPR. An informal email to the address above is sufficient. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.
